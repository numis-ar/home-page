# Long-distance buses and terminals in Argentina — news list

Press coverage collected for the context section of `DESCRIPTION.md`.
Collected 2026-09-27.  Most descriptions summarise search-result extracts;
entries marked **(read)** were opened and checked in full.  Figures are as
reported by each outlet and should be confirmed against the primary source
(CNRT, Boletín Oficial) before being quoted.

## 1. Decree 883/2024 — the deregulation itself

- **Official summary of the decree** (Argentina.gob.ar) **(read)**.  Companies
  set routes, schedules, prices and service duration freely; boarding and
  alighting points chosen by the company subject to local approval; a
  public online National Passenger Transport Registry; services not in the
  registry may not be sold; new operators authorised 5 days after
  registering ("silencio positivo").
  https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/se-desregula-el-transporte-automotor-de-larga-y-media-distancia-0
- **Text of Decree 883/2024** (Argentina.gob.ar normativa).

  rules and mixed cargo; free disability tickets left as an open question.

## 2. Consequences: terminals, stops and safety controls

- **Micros de larga distancia: riesgos y oportunidades** (El Cronista).
  Companies no longer have to use any terminal and may stop anywhere not
  prohibited; terminals were the control points, so pre-departure CNRT
  checks (distance, driver rest, breathalyser, inspection) weaken; ridership
  fell from 50 M (2016) to 22 M.
  https://www.cronista.com/economia-politica/micros-de-larga-distancia-cuales-son-los-riesgos-de-la-desregulacion-y-las-oportunidades/

## 3. Retiro terminal: politics and concession

- **Milei analiza desregular las terminales para sacarle el negocio a
  Otero** (La Política Online, 2024-07) **(read)**.  Companies could use
  their own depots (San Fernando, Barracas, Once) as terminals; Retiro is
  the only federally controlled terminal; Vía Bariloche (~40 % of
  long-distance traffic per the article) backs deregulation.
  https://www.lapoliticaonline.com/politica/larga-distancia-milei-analiza-desregular-los-micros-y-que-las-empresas-salgan-de-donde-quieran/
- **El increíble negocio detrás de la estación de Retiro** (Diego Cabot,
  via Notiar).  Fees for shops, parking, luggage, toilets and the "toque de
  dársena" paid every time a bus docks.
  https://www.notiar.com.ar/index.php/component/k2/129583-el-increible-negocio-detras-de-la-estacion-de-retiro-y-el-empresario-al-que-nadie-se-le-atreve-por-diego-cabot
- **Un grupo de empresarios busca desplazar al "zar de Retiro"** (La Nación,
  2025-09-19) and **Infobae** (2025-09-21).  Private initiative after 32

  buses (CNRT, ANAC, Yvera data).
  https://www.infobae.com/economia/2025/09/13/en-los-ultimos-doce-meses-viajaron-mas-personas-en-avion-que-en-micros-de-larga-distancia-dentro-de-la-argentina/

  Tribuno, 2026-07-20) **(read)**.  Bus peak January 2025 with 2.49 M
